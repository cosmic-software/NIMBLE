NIMBLE — BIBLIOGRAPHY
=====================
NAC Image Mosaic Builder for Landing-site Evaluation ("Lunar NearCam Image Mapper")
Compiled 2026-10-06. References are grouped by role. Cite [D1] and [1] in anything that shows
pixels from this tool.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. DATA PRODUCTS USED
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[D1] Robinson, M. S., and the LROC Team. LRO Camera Reduced Data Record (RDR) Products,
     Version 1.0, LRO-L-LROC-5-RDR-V1.0, volume LROLRC_2001, product family
     BDR/NAC_POLE/NAC_POLE_SOUTH (South Pole NAC Mosaic, 1 m/pixel), tile NAC_POLE_P892S2250.
     NASA Planetary Data System, Cartography & Imaging Sciences Node / LROC SOC, Arizona State
     University. https://data.lroc.im-ldi.com/lroc/view_rdr/NAC_POLE_SOUTH
     Archive: https://pds.lroc.im-ldi.com/data/LRO-L-LROC-5-RDR-V1.0/LROLRC_2001/

[D2] Robinson, M. S., and the LROC Team. LRO Camera Calibrated Data Record (CDR) Products,
     LRO-L-LROC-3-CDR-V1.0 (NAC frames listed in runs/<site>/STRIPS_IN_BOX.csv).
     NASA Planetary Data System. https://pds.lroc.im-ldi.com/data/LRO-L-LROC-3-CDR-V1.0/

[D3] PDS Geosciences Node, Washington University in St. Louis. Lunar Orbital Data Explorer
     (ODE) REST interface, footprint and metadata queries for LRO LROC CDRNAC4 products.
     https://oderest.rsl.wustl.edu/  (accessed 2026-10-06)
